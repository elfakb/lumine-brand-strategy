# Brand Voice — Lumïne

---

## Voice in One Sentence

Lumïne sounds like a knowledgeable friend who happens to know a lot about skincare —
honest, warm, never condescending, and always on your side.

---

## 5 Voice Adjectives

| Adjective | What It Means in Practice |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| **Honest** | We say what works and what doesn't — including our own limitations |
| **Warm** | We talk *with* our audience, not *at* them |
| **Educational** | We explain the why, not just the what |
| **Calm** | No hype, no urgency, no fear-based messaging |
| **Direct** | Short sentences. Clear claims. No filler. |

---

## What Lumïne Sounds Like

### Words We Use
- "your skin barrier"
- "here's what's actually in it"
- "you don't need this if..."
- "real results take time"
- "we tested this because..."
- "skip this step if your skin is..."
- "not for everyone — and that's okay"

### Words We Never Use
- "miracle"
- "luxury"
- "anti-aging"
- "flawless"
- "perfect skin"
- "game-changer"
- "you need this"
- "holy grail"

## Tone by Platform

### Instagram Feed
- Calm, editorial, educational
- Longer captions are fine — our audience reads
- Hook in line 1, value in the middle, soft CTA at the end
- Never salesy, never urgent

**Example:**
> Your skin barrier isn't broken — it's just overwhelmed.
> Three products. Eight weeks. That's all it took for Zeynep to see a difference.
> Here's exactly what she used and why it worked. ↓

---

### Instagram Stories
- Casual, conversational, real
- Can be imperfect — typos are fine, raw footage is fine
- Ask questions, run polls, share behind-the-scenes
- Feel like a text from a friend

**Example:**
> ok real question — how many products are you using rn?
> (be honest we won't judge)

---

### TikTok
- Fast, punchy, educational hooks
- First 2 seconds are everything
- Use trends but stay on-brand — we don't do sounds that feel off
- Show, don't just tell

**Example hook:**
> "The reason your moisturizer isn't working has nothing to do with the moisturizer."

---

### Caption Structure (Instagram)

```
Line 1:  Hook — a question, a bold statement, or a surprising fact
Line 2:  [blank line]
Line 3-6: The value — explain, educate, or tell a story
Line 7:  [blank line]
Line 8:  Soft CTA — not "buy now", but "save this", "tag someone", "tell us below"
Line 9:  [blank line]
Line 10: Hashtags (10–15, mixed size)
```

## Voice Don'ts

| Don't | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Don't use exclamation marks excessively | Feels cheap and untrustworthy |
| Don't write in all caps | Aggressive, not our vibe |
| Don't make skin sound like a problem to fix | Skin is not the enemy |
| Don't pressure with limited time offers | Manipulative, kills trust |
| Don't use before/after framing negatively | "Before" implies something is wrong |
| Don't speak to insecurities | We build confidence, not doubt |

---

## Voice Do's

| Do | Why |
|----|-----|
| Educate without overwhelming | Our audience is smart but busy |
| Acknowledge that skincare is personal | Not every product works for everyone |
| Be honest about what a product can't do | Builds long-term trust |
| Celebrate small wins | "Your skin looking a little calmer today counts" |
| Invite conversation | Ask questions, respond to comments |
| Use "we" sparingly | Brand voice, not corporate speak |

---

## Real vs. Fake Lumïne Voice

| Fake Lumïne | Real Lumïne |
|-------------|-------------|
| "This holy grail serum will transform your skin!" | "This serum does one thing really well. Here's what it is." |
| "Limited stock — get yours before it's gone!" | "We restock every month. No rush." |
| "Achieve flawless skin in 7 days." | "Skin takes time. We're in this with you." |
| "You NEED this in your routine." | "You might not need this. Here's how to know." |
| "Anti-aging formula for youthful skin." | "Supports your skin barrier as it changes over time." |

## Emoji Usage

- Used sparingly — max 1–2 per caption
- Only when it adds meaning, not decoration
- Preferred: 🌿 ✦ → ↓ · —
- Never: 🔥💥✨🙌 (too hype, off-brand)
