# 🎨 Visual Prompts — Lumïne

> Ready-to-use prompts for Midjourney and DALL-E.
> All prompts follow Lumïne's visual identity: sage green, lavender, mist blue, oat white.
> Mood: calm, minimal, clean, soft natural light.

---

## Brand Visual Identity

| Element | Direction |
|---------|-----------|
| **Color palette** | Sage green, soft lavender, mist blue, oat white, deep sage |
| **Lighting** | Soft natural light, morning window light, diffused |
| **Mood** | Calm, clean, minimal, honest |
| **Texture** | Linen, ceramic, glass, raw wood, stone |
| **Avoid** | Dark backgrounds, heavy shadows, neon colors, glitter, busy compositions |
