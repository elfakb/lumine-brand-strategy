# Content Pillars & Themes — Lumïne

---

## Overview

Lumïne's content is built on 5 pillars. Every single post belongs to one of these pillars.
This keeps the feed intentional, consistent, and strategically balanced.

| Pillar | Focus | % of Content |
|--------|-------|--------------|
| 01 — Education | Teach skincare science | 30% |
| 02 — Transparency | Show what's inside | 20% |
| 03 — Community | Real people, real skin | 20% |
| 04 — Lifestyle | Skincare as self-care | 20% |
| 05 — Product | What we make and why | 10% |

---

## Pillar 01 — Education

> *"We teach so our audience never has to guess."*

Lumïne's audience is curious and research-driven. They want to understand their skin,
not just be told what to buy. Education builds trust before any product is introduced.

### Content Themes

**Theme 1.1 — Ingredient Science**
Break down one ingredient per post. What it does, how it works,
who it's for, and what to pair it with (or avoid).
> Example: "Niacinamide 101 — what it actually does to your pores"

**Theme 1.2 — Routine Building**
Guide the audience through building a simple, effective routine
from scratch. Morning vs. night. Beginner vs. advanced.
> Example: "If you're new to skincare, start here. 3 products. That's it."

**Theme 1.3 — Myth Busting**
Address common skincare myths with science-backed answers.
Relatable hooks, honest conclusions.
> Example: "Drinking water doesn't hydrate your skin. Here's what actually does."

**Theme 1.4 — Skin Barrier Deep Dives**
The skin barrier is Lumïne's core educational topic.
Explain what it is, how it gets damaged, and how to repair it.
> Example: "Signs your skin barrier is damaged — and what to do about it"

## Pillar 02 — Transparency

> *"We show our work."*

Transparency is what separates Lumïne from greenwashing brands.
This pillar proves that our ingredients, processes, and decisions are open books.

### Content Themes

**Theme 2.1 — Ingredient Lists Explained**
Take a real Lumïne product, show the full ingredient list,
and explain what every single thing does.
> Example: "Every ingredient in our Barrier Repair Moisturizer — explained in plain English"

**Theme 2.2 — Why We Left Something Out**
Tell the audience what Lumïne chose NOT to include and why.
No fragrance. No alcohol. No fillers. Explain the reasoning.
> Example: "Why there's no fragrance in any Lumïne product. Ever."

**Theme 2.3 — Formula Development Stories**
Behind-the-scenes of how a product was developed.
What didn't work. What we changed. How long it took.
> Example: "It took us 14 formulas to get the SPF right. Here's why."

**Theme 2.4 — Sustainability Reports**
Show the real numbers. Packaging materials, carbon footprint,
recycling programs. No vague "we care about the planet" claims.
> Example: "Our packaging is 94% recyclable. Here's the 6% we're still working on."

## Pillar 03 — Community

> *"Our audience is our brand."*

Lumïne doesn't use influencers as the face of the brand.
Real customers, real results, real conversations — that's the content.

### Content Themes

**Theme 3.1 — Customer Skin Stories**
Real people sharing their skin journey with Lumïne products.
No before/after manipulation — just honest timelines.
> Example: "Zeynep's 8-week skin diary using only our Niacinamide Serum"

**Theme 3.2 — Q&A & Ask Me Anything**
Weekly or bi-weekly Q&A format. Answer real questions from
the audience about skincare, ingredients, routines.
> Example: "You asked, we answered — 10 skincare questions from our DMs this week"

**Theme 3.3 — Poll & Opinion Content**
Engage the audience with opinions and preferences.
Light, fun, conversation-starting content.
> Example: "SPF in your moisturizer or separate SPF — which team are you on?"

**Theme 3.4 — UGC Reposts & Spotlights**
Repost user-generated content with permission and credit.
Celebrate customers who share their routines.
> Example: "We love how @user incorporated our cleanser into her routine ✦"

## Pillar 04 — Lifestyle

> *"Skincare is a moment, not a chore."*

This pillar connects Lumïne to the broader life of our audience.
Morning routines, slow evenings, self-care without pressure.

### Content Themes

**Theme 4.1 — Morning Ritual Content**
The first 10 minutes of the day. Soft lighting, calm energy,
simple routine. Aspirational but achievable.
> Example: "My 4-minute morning routine for days when I have zero time"

**Theme 4.2 — Evening Wind-Down**
Nighttime routine as a reset. Not just skincare steps —
the feeling of washing off the day.
> Example: "Your skin repairs itself overnight. Here's how to help it."

**Theme 4.3 — Skin & Mental Health**
The connection between stress, sleep, diet and skin.
Gentle, non-prescriptive, empathetic content.
> Example: "Your skin breaking out during exam season isn't your fault"

**Theme 4.4 — Seasonal Skin Content**
How skin changes with seasons and how to adapt the routine.
Timely, relevant, practical.
> Example: "Winter is coming. Here's what that means for your skin barrier."

## Pillar 05 — Product

> *"We talk about our products last — because trust comes first."*

Product content is kept to 10% deliberately.
By the time the audience sees a product post, they already trust us.

### Content Themes

**Theme 5.1 — Product Spotlights**
One product, deep focus. What it does, who it's for,
how to use it, what to expect in week 1 vs. week 8.
> Example: "Everything you need to know about our Niacinamide Serum"

**Theme 5.2 — How To Use**
Step-by-step application guides. Texture, amount, layering order.
Visual and practical.
> Example: "How much SPF do you actually need? (Most people use too little)"

**Theme 5.3 — Product Comparisons**
Honest comparison of Lumïne products vs. alternatives.
Not "we're better" — but "here's how we're different."
> Example: "Lumïne SPF vs. 3 other SPFs we respect — honest comparison"

**Theme 5.4 — New Product Teasers**
Behind-the-scenes of upcoming products. Ingredient hints,
texture previews, community voting on names or shades.
> Example: "We've been working on something for dry skin for 6 months. First look ↓"
