## Persona 1 — Zeynep (Primary)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Zeynep K. |
| **Age** | 21 |
| **Occupation** | University student (Psychology) |
| **Location** | Istanbul, Turkey |
| **Income** | Low-mid (part-time job, family support) |
| **Platforms** | TikTok, Instagram, YouTube |

### Her Life
Zeynep spends most of her day between classes, the library, and her phone. She's been dealing with acne since she was 16 and has tried everything — from drugstore cleansers to expensive serums she saw on TikTok. Most of them either didn't work or made things worse. She's skeptical now. She doesn't trust brands that over-promise.

She follows skincare creators who show real skin — not filtered, not retouched. She's done a lot of research. She knows what niacinamide is. She's read about the skin barrier. But she's still confused because there's too much information and too many conflicting opinions.

### Goals
- Clear up post-acne marks without breaking the bank
- Build a simple, consistent skincare routine
- Find a brand she can actually trust

### Pain Points
- Overwhelmed by too many products and steps
- Has damaged her skin barrier from over-exfoliating
- Doesn't understand which ingredients conflict with each other
- Feels like most brands are lying to her
- Budget is tight — can't afford to waste money on products that don't work

### What She Distrusts
- "Miracle" or "overnight" results
- Before/after photos that look obviously edited
- Brands that don't list full ingredients
- Influencers who promote everything
- "Luxury" packaging with weak formulas

### Content She Engages With
- "What I actually use for acne scars" style TikToks
- Dermatologist explainer videos
- Ingredient breakdown carousels on Instagram
- Honest reviews with bad results included
- "My skin after 30 days of..." content

### Her Quote
> "I just want something that works and doesn't cost a fortune. Is that too much to ask?"

## Persona 2 — Ece (Secondary)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Ece B. |
| **Age** | 24 |
| **Occupation** | Junior UX Designer |
| **Location** | Ankara, Turkey |
| **Income** | Mid (first full-time job) |
| **Platforms** | Instagram, Pinterest, Substack |

### Her Life
Ece just started her first real job and is navigating the stress of adulthood. She's noticed her skin reacting to stress more than it used to — breakouts around her jaw, dullness, uneven texture. She cares about what she puts on her skin and in her body. She reads labels. She knows what greenwashing is and she's annoyed by it.

She's aesthetically minded — her Instagram is curated, she appreciates good design. She would buy a product partly because the packaging looks good on her shelf. But she also needs it to actually work. She's willing to spend more on fewer, better products.

### Goals
- Build a minimal, effective routine that fits her busy schedule
- Find brands that are genuinely sustainable, not just aesthetically "clean"
- Manage stress-related breakouts and dullness

### Pain Points
- Can't tell which brands are truly clean vs. just marketing
- Gets frustrated by complicated routines she can't stick to
- Has fragrance allergies — most "natural" brands still use essential oils
- Wants sustainable packaging but doesn't want to sacrifice efficacy

### What She Distrusts
- Brands that use "natural" as a marketing word without substance
- Essential oils hidden in ingredient lists
- Vague sustainability claims without proof
- Over-designed packaging that's not recyclable
- Brands that feel like they're targeting 35+ women, not her

### Content She Engages With
- Aesthetic flat-lay product photography
- "What's actually in this product" deep dives
- Minimalist routine content ("my 3-step morning routine")
- Sustainability reports and brand transparency posts
- Pinterest-worthy lifestyle content

### Her Quote
> "I want a brand that respects my intelligence and my skin at the same time."
