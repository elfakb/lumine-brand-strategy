## Founding Story

Lumïne was born out of frustration.

Too many products. Too many promises. Too many ingredients that nobody could pronounce.

The founders — two 23-year-olds tired of their bathroom shelves overflowing with half-used serums — decided to strip everything back. What does skin actually need? What actually works? What can we prove?

The answer was Lumïne: a skincare brand built on radical simplicity, full ingredient transparency, and the belief that **you don't need 12 steps to have good skin.**

## Market Positioning

```
BUDGET                                              LUXURY
  |----The Ordinary----|----Lumïne----|----Tatcha----|
                    accessible
                    premium
```

Lumïne sits between **The Ordinary** (budget, clinical) and **CeraVe** (drugstore, medical feel) — but with a warmer, more personal brand identity that speaks directly to Gen Z.

---

## What Makes Lumïne Different

- **Ingredient transparency** — QR code on every product linking to full formula breakdown
- **Skip-care philosophy** — actively tells customers to buy *less*
- **Dermatologist-reviewed** formulas, not just "dermatologist-tested"
- **No fragrance, ever** — a non-negotiable brand rule
- **Community-first** — UGC over influencer campaigns
